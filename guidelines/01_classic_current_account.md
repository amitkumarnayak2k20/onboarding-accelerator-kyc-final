# Classic Current Account — training summary

## Public product context
Lloyds describes the Classic Account as free everyday banking when the customer stays in credit. Current-account applications are subject to an assessment of the applicant's circumstances.

## Publicly described application information
The Lloyds account-opening guide asks for personal details, contact information, address/residency information and financial/employment information. Most current accounts require applicants to be 18 or over. Identity checks are performed; most app applicants will usually need a valid UK or non-UK passport or current UK photo driving licence.

## Suggested demo intake checklist
- Applicant's legal name and date of birth
- Current UK residential address
- Email and telephone
- Nationality and tax residency as applicable
- Employment status, occupation and employer where applicable
- Income, savings, regular outgoings and expected account funding/use
- Identity evidence
- Address evidence if the journey requests it

## Demo verification rules
- Compare identity-document name and DOB with application values.
- Check the document is not expired where an expiry field is available.
- Compare address evidence with the declared address when supplied.
- Missing or inconsistent evidence => `NEEDS_MORE_INFORMATION` or `MANUAL_REVIEW`, with explicit reason codes.

Do not infer approval from meeting this checklist. Use current Lloyds application criteria and controls for real applications.
