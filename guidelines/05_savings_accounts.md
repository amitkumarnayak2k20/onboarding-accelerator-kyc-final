# Savings accounts — training summary

## Public product context
Lloyds offers multiple savings products with different terms, eligibility and access conditions. The public qualifying-current-accounts page states that Club Lloyds, Reward or Advantage savings accounts require one of the listed qualifying current accounts. This does not mean every Lloyds savings product has the same prerequisite.

## Demo intake checklist
- Applicant identity and DOB
- Residential address
- Existing Lloyds current-account relationship, if the selected savings product requires one
- Selected savings product name
- Funding source and intended deposit information where requested by the real product journey
- Tax residency information where required

## Demo verification rules
- Verify identity and address consistency.
- Capture the exact savings product selected.
- If the product has a qualifying-current-account prerequisite, check the applicant's declared account type against the uploaded, maintained demo allow-list.
- Mark relationship checks as `SELF_DECLARED_NOT_VERIFIED` unless connected to an approved bank system.
- Do not infer interest rates, minimum balances, withdrawal restrictions or ISA tax eligibility from this document. Retrieve the latest product terms.

Product-specific savings terms change frequently. The live Lloyds product page and terms prevail.
