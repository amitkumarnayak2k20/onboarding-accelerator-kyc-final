# Joint Current Account — training summary

## Public eligibility points
Lloyds' public joint-account page states both applicants must:
- Be aged 18 or over.
- Be UK residents.
- Each hold one or more eligible existing Lloyds products (the public page lists current account, savings account, credit card, loan, mortgage or share-dealing account).
The page says student and youth accounts cannot be made into joint accounts. Check the live Lloyds journey for the current process and eligible products.

## Demo intake checklist
Collect the following separately for Applicant 1 and Applicant 2:
- Full legal name and DOB
- Residential address and residency
- Identity evidence
- Address evidence if required
- Existing Lloyds product type (self-declared in this demo)
- Consent to joint application

## Demo verification rules
- Verify each applicant independently using their own identity evidence.
- Compare each applicant's name and DOB against their own document.
- Check both applicants meet the stated age/residency conditions.
- Check both declare an eligible existing Lloyds product; this is not externally verified in the demo.
- Never treat one applicant's documents as evidence for the other applicant.
- If either applicant fails a check, do not mark the joint application as passed.

The demo does not establish legal authority, account mandate, financial association or actual product eligibility.
