# General personal account opening — training summary

## Purpose
Collect consistent applicant information and evidence before the bank's own identity and eligibility checks.

## Applicant information to collect
- Full legal name and any previous names
- Date of birth
- Nationality, country of birth and residency / immigration information where relevant
- Current residential address and postcode
- Previous address details if requested by the live application journey
- Contact details
- Employment status, occupation, employer and start date
- Income, savings, regular outgoings and expected account funding/use
- Tax residency and relevant tax identification numbers where required by the live process
- Account purpose and expected use

## Identity evidence
Lloyds' public guidance says most current-account applicants using the app will usually need a valid UK or non-UK passport or current UK photo driving licence. Other journeys and in-branch applications may have different accepted-document rules. Existing customers may be able to verify using their Lloyds chip-and-PIN bank card. If primary evidence is unavailable, follow the live Lloyds proof-of-identity list; do not invent alternatives.

## Address evidence
The Lloyds public pages list examples such as utility bills and bank statements in relevant circumstances. Acceptance depends on the specific journey and document rules. Check document name, address, issue date, legibility and whether the evidence type is currently accepted.

## Agent checks (demo rules only)
1. Confirm required fields and expected documents are present.
2. Extract fields from the identity document.
3. Compare extracted full name and date of birth to the application.
4. Check document expiry if an expiry date exists.
5. Extract the address from address evidence and compare it to the declared address.
6. Record each field-level result and reason.
7. Assign a preliminary risk band using explicit demo rules.
8. Do not claim document authenticity or government-register verification from text extraction alone.
9. Escalate ambiguous, low-confidence, conflicting or unsupported evidence.

## Important
The agent's output is a preliminary training result, not a Lloyds account approval. Live policy, fraud controls, AML/sanctions screening, credit checks and authorised bank decisioning are outside this document.
